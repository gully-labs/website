## Content policy (draft)

You may pin and upload only content you have the right to publish. The following is not allowed on Gully IPFS:

1. Sexual content involving minors, in any form.
2. Other illegal content, including terrorism, human trafficking and credible threats.
3. Malware, phishing and anything built to steal credentials or funds.
4. Other people's private information, and sexual images shared without the person's consent.
5. Content that infringes copyright or trademark. We act on valid notices.
6. Licensed third-party assets you may not redistribute (for example game-engine asset store content).
7. [Owner decision: adult content that is legal and consensual: allowed with labelling, restricted, or not allowed.]

How we enforce it:

- Uploaded and pinned images are scored automatically. Video, SVG, large images and anything the scanner flags or
  cannot score are reviewed by a person. [Hash matching against known child abuse material: planned, see the
  application draft.]
- Anyone can report content with the report form. A person reads every report, the most serious first. A report
  does not remove anything by itself.
- Content that breaks this policy is taken down: unpinned from every Gully node, refused by the gateway, and the same
  file is refused if it is uploaded again under any CID. The account may be suspended or closed. Serious cases are
  reported to the authorities as the law requires.
- A takedown is by CID. Content you pinned stays reachable on other IPFS nodes that hold it; we cannot remove it from
  nodes we do not run.
