**Subject:** Commercial licence request: hosted Blockscout explorers for app chains (Gully Labs)

Hello Blockscout team,

I am writing from Gully Labs (https://gullylabs.xyz, X: @Gully_Labs). We build tools for onchain stock tokens and app chains, and we run branded block explorers for Arbitrum Orbit and other EVM app chains under the name Chain Kit. Each explorer is a Blockscout deployment, configured with the customer's name, logo and colours, hosted and operated by us.

We would like a commercial licence under LicenseRef-Blockscout 1.0 so that we can offer hosted, managed explorers to paying customers. Until we have one, we are not taking orders; Chain Kit is on a waitlist.

Could you tell us:

1. **Pricing.** How is a commercial licence priced for us: per explorer, per chain, a flat annual fee, or a revenue share? Are there tiers by chain size or traffic?
2. **White-label limits.** What branding changes are allowed? Where must "Powered by Blockscout" appear (footer, about page, both), and may customers change its size or colour?
3. **Our own operations.** Does it count as managed use when we run the stack ourselves on our servers for a customer, as opposed to the customer running it on their own infrastructure? Would a customer who self-hosts with our setup scripts and support need their own licence?
4. **Our deployment layer.** We keep a small configuration layer (compose files, chain profiles, branding and a provisioning script) on top of unmodified upstream code. Is sharing that layer with a customer allowed under the licence, and what must it keep?
5. **Term and changes.** What are the term, renewal and termination conditions, and what happens to live explorers if terms change?

We are happy to share a short description of our setup, expected number of explorers (a handful in the first year) and any references you need. A call is fine if it is easier.

Thank you for your time.
